Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Shivendra Singh. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit it.

What this site collects

Nothing, by design. The site has no contact form, no accounts, no comments, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts. It does not set cookies. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies.

Server logs

Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, the pages you requested, your browser type and the time of the request. These logs are used by the host to run and secure the service. I do not use them to identify visitors. See your host's own privacy notice for details on how long they keep them.

Links to other sites

The site links to GitHub and LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy policies and collect information under their own rules once you open them.

If you contact me

If you send me a message on LinkedIn, I receive whatever you write and whatever your profile shows. I use it only to reply to you and I do not share it with anyone.

Your rights

Because I do not collect personal data through this site, there is nothing for me to delete or export. If you contacted me and want your message removed from my records, send me a message on LinkedIn and I will delete it.

Changes

If I add anything that changes this, such as analytics or a contact form, I will update this page and the date above before it goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message me on LinkedIn.
